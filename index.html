All About Dr Tushar Chandak Family Dental Care

Healthy Smiles • Gentle Care • Advanced Treatment

Our Specialities

Comprehensive Dental Services

State-of-the-art restorative, aesthetic, and preventive dental solutions personalized for every member of your family.

Cosmetic

Teeth Whitening

Professional in-clinic laser & LED teeth whitening to eliminate stubborn stains from coffee, tea, and tobacco, brightening your smile by up to 8 shades in a single comfortable session.

  • Instant, visible results in 45 minutes
  • Enamel-safe, zero dental sensitivity formula
Surgery

Dental Implants

Permanent, titanium tooth roots surgically placed into the jawbone, crowned with lifelike zirconia caps to restore missing teeth with 100% natural biting force and lifelong durability.

  • Permanent solution for missing teeth
  • Preserves jawbone structure & facial contours
Cosmetic

Veneers & Smile Makeover

Custom-crafted, ultra-thin porcelain and composite shells bonded to the front teeth to conceal gaps, chipped enamel, severe discoloration, and slight misalignments for a Hollywood smile.

  • Stain-resistant natural porcelain finish
  • Customized to facial symmetry and smile line
Restorative

Root Canal Treatment (RCT)

Advanced painless rotary endodontic therapy that cleans infected nerve tissue, disinfects the root canals, and seals the tooth hermetically to save infected teeth from extraction.

  • Fast relief from severe tooth pain & infection
  • Single-sitting RCT options available
Orthodontics

Orthodontics & Braces

Correction of crooked teeth, crowding, overbites, and gaps using metal braces, ceramic aesthetic braces, and invisible clear aligners for teenagers and adults alike.

  • Discreet clear aligners and ceramic brackets
  • Corrects bites, jaw alignment, and spacing
Restorative

Crowns & Fixed Bridges

High-strength Zirconia, E-Max, and porcelain-fused caps custom sculpted to protect fractured teeth post-root canal or span the gap created by one or more missing teeth.

  • 100% metal-free biocompatible Zirconia
  • Precision digital CAD/CAM milling
Pediatric

Kids Dentistry (Pediatric)

Child-centered dental care focused on positive early experiences. We offer painless milk tooth fillings, pulpectomy, fluoride varnishes, pit & fissure sealants, and habit breakers.

  • Fear-free, cheerful child environment
  • Preventive caries control and guidance
Restorative

Dentures & Partial Prosthetics

Lightweight, flexible, and cast partial dentures providing comfortable chewing and clear speech for patients missing multiple teeth without needing extensive surgery.

  • Snug fit with unbreakable flexible resin
  • Restores full chewing power & facial fullness
Surgery

Oral Surgery & Wisdom Tooth

Minimally invasive surgical extractions of impacted third molars (wisdom teeth), root tip removals, apicoectomies, and cyst treatments executed with gentle local anesthesia.

  • Painless wisdom tooth impaction removal
  • Rapid healing with minimal post-op swelling
Restorative

Fillings & Dental Sealants

Preventive sealants to block cavities in deep grooves and tooth-colored composite resin fillings that repair tooth decay seamlessly without ugly silver mercury amalgams.

  • 100% Mercury-free, aesthetic composite
  • Stops cavity progression instantly
Preventive

Teeth Cleaning & Scaling

Ultrasonic scaling to dislodge hardened calculus, bacterial tartar, and stains from coffee and nicotine, preventing gum disease, bone loss, and bad breath (halitosis).

  • Gentle ultrasonic piezotechnology
  • Keeps gums tight, healthy, and pink
Restorative

Complete Full Arch Dentures

Ergonomically engineered full upper and lower dentures designed to restore natural facial profile, clear pronunciation, and chewing stability for senior citizens.

  • Suction-fit high impact acrylic
  • Natural tooth shading & gum stippling
Periodontics

Gum Surgery & Periodontics

Specialized periodontic treatments including flap surgeries, bone grafting, and gingival depigmentation to eliminate bleeding gums, loose teeth, and deep infected periodontal pockets.

  • Stops tooth mobility and bleeding gums
  • Regenerative bone & tissue grafting
Preventive

Scaling & Airflow Polishing

High-pressure micro-polishing that buffs the enamel surfaces smooth, removing microscopic discolorations and leaving your teeth feeling silky and noticeably glossy.

  • Super-smooth gloss enamel feel
  • Recommended every 6 months for optimum hygiene
Diagnostics

Digital RVG X-Ray

Immediate computerized radiovisiography with 90% less radiation exposure than conventional film X-rays, providing razor-sharp digital diagnosis of hidden cavities, bone height, and root canals.

  • Instant digital display within seconds
  • Safe for children & expectant mothers
Preventive

Routine Dental Checkups

Comprehensive 360-degree oral examination, soft tissue screening, bite inspection, and hygiene coaching to detect oral issues early before they develop into expensive painful emergencies.

  • Thorough dental wellness checkup
  • Personalized preventive oral health plan
Restorative

Tooth Coloured Fillings

Nano-hybrid composite materials shade-matched to your natural teeth, creating an invisible, strong bond that strengthens the remaining tooth structure and prevents recurring decay.

  • Invisible seamless shade matching
  • Chemically bonds to restore tooth strength
